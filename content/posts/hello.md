This is the first post on the English side of hancic.site.

## Why another blog

The Chinese blog at [blog.hancic.site](https://blog.hancic.site) has been running for a while.
Some of the topics — distributed systems, trading systems, and engineering practice —
are interesting to a global audience too. So here we are: an English blog, written
without a translation step.

## What to expect

- Systems engineering and architecture notes
- Trading systems and quant tooling
- Occasional tooling and self-hosting stories
